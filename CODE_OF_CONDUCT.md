# Code of conduct

Hoverbox is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to make it a friendly and respectful place.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what gets merged, and that "not right now" is a valid answer.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about anyone's identity, background or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address or email, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging others to do any of the above.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer privately on GitHub, [@aswin099](https://github.com/aswin099). Reports are kept confidential. Please include links or screenshots if you can.

## What happens next

The maintainer will review every report and decide on a response, which can range from a private note, to removing comments, to blocking someone from the project. Everyone is treated fairly, including the person reported.

This applies to all project spaces, and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.
